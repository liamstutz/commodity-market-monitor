Rule, costs and limits

The 12-period EMA uses completed five-minute closes and resets after missing candles and each New York calendar day. At least twelve consecutive bars are required. A close above the EMA at the selected anchor chooses long; below chooses short; an exact tie makes no trade. The next bar's open is the hypothetical entry. Exit uses the open after the chosen holding period. Any missing candle through exit excludes the trade.

09:30 New York is an experimental anchor, not the exchange open for every commodity. The $5 round-trip fee and one tick of slippage per side are editable assumptions, not a broker quote. Each replay uses one full-size CL, NG or GC contract; it excludes financing, margin, liquidity constraints and actual bid/ask spreads. The always-buy benchmark uses the identical entries, exits and costs.

The earlier 70% and later 30% split is chronological. The later sample is not independently out of sample if you tune settings after seeing it. Short, overlapping samples do not establish profitability. Drawdown uses closed trades only, so it understates possible intratrade losses.

03 / OBSERVED AFTER STARTUP

A separate forward watch

FIXED 09:30 ANCHOR

Only signals received within 30 minutes of the completed 09:30 New York candle are saved here. The baseline is the latest completed close actually available at observation time. The target is one hour after observation, not one hour after the historical signal. This is a directional watch, not simulated fills or P&L. It does not change when you edit replay settings.

The first available close on or after the target is used, with a maximum 30-minute tolerance. Missing outcomes become unscored. Flat moves count as misses. First-issued values and resolved outcomes are kept.
