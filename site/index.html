20-OBSERVATION MEAN REVERSION

A fixed rule looks for prices or spreads more than one standard deviation from their recent mean. Each card shows exactly why it is active, neutral or paused.

Historical diagnostic & model assumptions

The historical check uses the signal from two observations earlier to predict the next observed change. It uses revised spot data and does not reconstruct publication times, executable prices, contract rolls or trading costs. The “always up” benchmark uses the same active dates. Flat moves count as misses.
